# Early Opportunity 16-step framework

Product source of truth for `/invest/early-opp`. Typed constants live in `src/lib/analysis/early-opp/steps.ts`.

This is a **decision aid**, not personalized investment advice. InvestSalsa never auto-buys. Missing Financial Modeling Prep figures stay unknown — they are not invented.

## Process

Space → leader → financials → moat → entry / kill switch. Catch a secular spend wave, then the S-curve, before the crowd.

## 16 steps

1. **Know yourself** — temperament and blind spots first. Not scored from market data.
2. **Secular trend** — play where the puck is going. Theme first; ticker second.
3. **Catch the S-curve early** — position before the crowd. Flat → vertical can repeat.
4. **Follow the money** — quantify CapEx / future spend from loaded statements.
5. **Who gets the CapEx** — map the stack (accelerators, memory, interconnect, other). Do not wait for the P&L to prove it.
6. **Identify winners** — quality, roadmaps, switching costs, investment intensity.
7. **Financials** — revenue growth, **FCF growth (golden)**, margin trends. Avoid sales up and margins collapsing.
8. **Financial kill switches** — cash burn without share gains; serial dilution; forever “adjusted” accounting; debt vs revenue / profit.
9. **CapEx quality / ROIC** — rising CapEx into a demand wave can be bullish if ROIC holds.
10. **Moat** — can it be copied in ≤3 years? If yes, not a moat.
11. **Ownership / smart money** — insider buys vs dumps; 13F is a soft signal only. Do not invent holder names.
12. **Analyst targets** — sentiment only. Revision direction matters more than a price target.
13. **PEG** — P/E ÷ expected earnings growth. Under 1 can be cheap on growth — only when PEG actually loaded.
14. **Chart timing after fundamentals** — fundamentals = what; chart = when. Do not buy a broken thesis because it looks cheap.
15. **Why is it moving (or stuck)** — if you do not know why, you do not have an edge.
16. **Exit, size, kill switch before entry** — write the exit at entry. Size the flyer. Kill when the thesis changes.

## Status language (product)

- **Pass** — loaded evidence supports the step.
- **Soft** — mixed or only a proxy.
- **Fail** — loaded evidence works against the step.
- **Unknown** — inputs missing, educational-only, or qualitative read unavailable.

## Honesty

- Never invent FMP numbers, leftover cash, or a buy/sell order.
- Qualitative steps may use AI when `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` is set. Without the key, those steps stay on statements and show unknown where a theme/moat/why cannot be scored.
- Product UI does not surface desk leftover bars on this page.
